THE DREAMTALES PROJECT

Bedtime, imagined together.

Your idea begins a five-scene story. The first two scenes are prepared before playback. While you listen, upcoming scenes are prepared in the background. New ideas join the next scene that has not yet been written. Switch off “Keep telling the story” to turn pages yourself.

Natural narration uses Kokoro when installed; otherwise a local device voice is offered. Ambient rain, wind, crickets, waves, and optional gentle thunder are synthesized on this device. Scene illustrations use a hosted image model when configured, with local drawings as a fallback.

Stories are stored temporarily on this device. When hosted illustrations are enabled, screened scene text, character names, and settings are sent to OpenAI to create each image. Obvious contact details are removed. Requests and generated stories are screened for unsuitable content, including alcohol and drugs. This is an early prototype with limited moderation, intended for supervised use.
